About Me

I am an undergraduate student at Tsinghua University, majoring in Automation. My research interests lie at the intersection of Embodied AI, Reinforcement Learning, and Generative Models (Diffusion/Flow-matching).

I am particularly interested in building generalizable Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies that can operate robustly in the physical world. Currently, I am working on bridging the gap between high-level semantic reasoning and low-level contact-rich control.
